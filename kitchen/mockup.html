Garage + hall + kitchen · layout mockup · Oct 15 2026

Candelaria Kitchen

The garage, the hall beside it and the old kitchen as one room of about 680 sq ft. Cooking and storage run along the west side with a 12 ft island in front. The old garage door becomes a glass wall, with the dining table in front of it. A coffee bar and nook sit by the porch window, next to the door from the foyer.

What goes where

  1. A

    Cook wall. A 36 in induction range under a plaster hood. Honed stone slab from the counter to the ceiling, with oak drawers below and no upper cabinets.

  2. B

    Tall wall, on the old hall's wall: column fridge and freezer, two wall ovens and two pantries. Floor-to-ceiling rift oak with no handles.

  3. C

    Island, 12'-0" × 4'-6", with waterfall quartz ends. Prep sink, dishwasher and trash face the cook wall, and four stools sit on the far side.

  4. D

    Dining for eight, in front of the old garage door opening. It becomes a 16 ft four-panel glass slider onto the driveway apron.

  5. E

    Coffee bar and nook by the foyer door: a beverage fridge and open shelves, plus a banquette under the porch window.

  6. F

    Sideboard under the old garage's porch window, for serving and bar storage.

  7. G

    Stair (existing). Its foot stays open, and the path to it runs past the tall wall.

Assumed

  • The old kitchen's west wall is drawn square at 21'-5". The floor plan's slant isn't confirmed yet.
  • The garage floor is raised to the house floor, with an 8 ft ceiling throughout.
  • Water, power, gas and the hood's duct go wherever the layout wants them, not where they are now.
  • The garage's west wall comes out, with a beam in the attic (dashed). An engineer sizes it and decides its south support: a post in the fridge's end panel, or a beam along the old kitchen wall.
